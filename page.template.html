Why it cannot be faked

The seal is a hash of the compressed build. Change one byte of what is inside and the seal stops matching, and this page tells you so. Nobody has to be trusted — the check is re-run in front of you, on your own machine.

It is still a real PNG. The build rides in the text chunks every PNG is allowed to carry, so a picture viewer shows a picture and ignores the rest. Nothing is hidden or smuggled — the chunks are named, and the whole point is that anybody can look.

Nothing leaves your browser. Compressing, hashing and reading all happen here. There is no server to send it to.
